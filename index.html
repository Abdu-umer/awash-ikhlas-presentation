Refining the Vision: A Strategic Response

Thank you for your precise and constructive feedback. For this revision, we focused our design process entirely on your three core directives:

01 / Objective

Radical Simplification

Streamlining the geometry to ensure clean, memorable marks that guarantee flawless practical application across digital screens, print, and 3D signage.

02 / Objective

Distinct Color & Typography

Expanding beyond a purely monochromatic palette to introduce a premium, modern aesthetic that gives Ikhlas its own unique character.

03 / Objective

The 20/80 Balance

Ensuring 20% of the design visually roots the brand in the trusted Awash heritage, while 80% establishes a fresh, innovative, and highly distinct identity for Ikhlas in the Islamic banking sector.

Concept 01 — Architectural Mark

Concept 1: The Monolithic Archway

Primary Daylight
Monolithic Blue
Reverse VIP / Nocturnal
White Luminescence
01 / Clarity

Absolute Clarity & Structural Trust

Addressing the request for ultimate simplicity, we distilled the archway into its purest geometric form. By utilizing a flat, monolithic structure in corporate Awash blue, this logo projects unshakeable financial stability and absolute clarity.

02 / Metaphor

The Conceptual "Window"

The intersection of the vertical pillar and the horizontal foundational shape creates a literal "window" in the negative space. This visualizes Awash Bank's IFB "window model" operating seamlessly within its conventional branches.

03 / Monogram

Dual Monogram Integration

The geometry cleverly conceals a hidden monogram acting as the 20% connection to the parent brand: the overarching pointed structure forms an "A" for Awash, sheltering the central vertical "I" for Ikhlas.

Optimized for physical cut-out letters, stone etching, and debossed stationery Slide 03 / 10
Concept 02 — Fluid Momentum

Concept 2: The Dynamic Flow

Primary Daylight
Blue & Gold
Reverse VIP / Nocturnal
White & Luminous Gold
01 / Growth

The River of Growth & Purity

Representing a complete creative leap to ensure we have a distinct array of options, this concept introduces fluid, ascending vertical waves. It captures the literal meaning of Ikhlas (purity) while symbolizing ethical financial growth, community nurture, and the life-giving Awash River heritage.

02 / Core

Premium Distinctiveness & Sharia Core

The central ascending gold pillar represents the pure, Sharia-compliant core of the IFB service, nurtured and protected by the surrounding blue waves. The integration of gold elevates the brand, signaling wealth preservation and high-value ethical banking.

03 / Heritage

Subtle Corporate DNA

The 20% connection to the parent bank is elegantly subtle. The two outer blue arcs frame the design into a gentle globe silhouette, while the fluid inner lines evoke the movement and vital flow of the iconic "Awash River."

Progressive kinetic identity built for digital screens and mobile interfaces Slide 04 / 10

Concept 3: The Refined Woven Sphere

Primary Daylight

01 / Refinement

Distinct Heritage, Streamlined for Tomorrow

We radically simplified our earlier woven concept by removing complex overlapping shadows, making it a bold, flat mark highly optimized for 3D physical signage and responsive digital scaling.

02 / Balance

The 20/80 Balance in Action

The outer circular boundary acts as the 20% connection, serving as a clear structural homage to the original Awash Bank globe. The 80% fresh identity is driven by dynamic inner lines forming the Arabic word "Ikhlas" (إخلاص).

03 / Palette

Expanded Color Architecture

Answering the call for a more distinctive identity, this concept breaks away from a monochromatic approach by introducing a striking Gold accent alongside trusted Awash Blue. This creates a premium, highly recognizable look that stands out in the IFB market.
